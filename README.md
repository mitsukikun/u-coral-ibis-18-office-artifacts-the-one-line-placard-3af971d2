# Office Artifacts: The One-Line Placard Contest

A one-line museum placard for an ordinary office printer, written as if it were an ancient artifact: the Oracle of the Paper Age, to which petitioners fed paper offerings and waited in silence for its verdict — on permanent loan from IT, which never came to collect it.

> Printer, beige plastic, c. 1998. Oracle of the Paper Age; petitioners fed it paper offerings and waited in silence for its verdict. On permanent loan from IT.
